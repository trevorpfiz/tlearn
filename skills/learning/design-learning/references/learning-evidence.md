# Learning evidence and adaptations

Read only the section relevant to a rationale, source check, or design disagreement. These sources inform tlearn; none has evaluated tlearn itself. The SKILL files contain our operational adaptations, not a reproduction of a book or a universal recipe.

## Math Academy foundation

[The Math Academy Way](https://www.justinmath.com/books/) by Justin Skycak, advised by Jason Roberts, is the central design influence. The [PDF](https://www.justinmath.com/files/the-math-academy-way.pdf) inspected for the repository is the working draft updated August 13, 2026. Useful locations in that version are mastery learning (pp. 209–214), micro-scaffolding and expertise reversal (pp. 219–223), layering (p. 243), interleaving (pp. 271–280), and targeted remediation (pp. 299–304). Page references may change as the draft evolves.

Its lesson design emphasizes small supported steps, practiced foundations, and progress toward independent use. Its diagnostic, adaptive scheduling, and long-term review systems are broader than tlearn's initial scope. We borrow instructional decisions while providing foundations by default, leaving navigation open, and limiting claims to observed task performance.

Skycak's [biology account](https://www.justinmath.com/q-and-a-4/) describes short instruction-and-exercise loops and coordinating foundations across relevant branches. It also describes LLM drift and the need for curation. This is a practitioner experience, not a controlled comparison or evidence for a fixed dose of questions. tlearn uses sourced, authored lesson content rather than requiring the learner to continually coach a live tutor.

## Active practice

[Chi and Wylie (2014), ICAP](https://education.asu.edu/sites/default/files/lcl/chiwylie2014icap_2.pdf), especially the engagement definitions on pp. 221–223, distinguish manipulating provided content from generating additional explanations or inferences. The framework motivates predictions and reasoning around visuals. Visible behavior is an imperfect proxy for thought, and a button click does not guarantee a particular engagement level. Direct explanation remains useful preparation for an attempt.

## Scaffold and fade

[Atkinson, Renkl, and Merrill (2003)](https://asu.elsevierpure.com/en/publications/transitioning-from-studying-examples-to-solving-problems-effects-/), abstract and experimental findings, studied fading worked steps and self-explanation prompts. Their two experiments support combining fading with attention to underlying principles; fading alone had less consistent far-transfer effects. tlearn adapts this into examples, completion tasks, and fresh independent tasks. Do not assume an identical sequence or transfer benefit for every biological skill.

The book's targeted-remediation discussion motivates connecting a mistake to a relevant component and checking the content when struggle recurs. For a static tool, feedback links and authored retries are sufficient; a diagnosis or adaptive assessment engine is not required.

## Fluency and layering

Skycak's [automaticity discussion](https://www.justinmath.com/cognitive-science-of-learning-developing-automaticity/) explains how reliable component operations can free attention for more complex reasoning. His [layering essay](https://www.justinmath.com/layering-building-structural-integrity-in-knowledge/) emphasizes active use of earlier knowledge in advanced work. These motivate recurring, relevant practice. They do not establish automaticity from a short lesson or justify universal time limits. Decide whether recall, a procedure, or authentic reference use supports the particular outcome.

## Retrieve and mix

[Karpicke and Blunt (2011)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21252317/), abstract and study report, found retrieval benefits over elaborative concept mapping in their science-text tasks, including inference questions. This supports attempting recall rather than only revisiting a representation. It does not make knowledge graphs useless: the graph organizes the lesson, while exercises supply practice.

Skycak's [interleaving discussion](https://www.justinmath.com/cognitive-science-of-learning-interleaving/) distinguishes initial similar practice from subsequent mixing that requires selecting a method. Apply this when discrimination matters; arbitrary mixing is not inherently better. Within-tool revisits are useful design choices, not a substitute for practice distributed across days. Longitudinal scheduling is deferred.

## Scientific reasoning

The [4C/ID model](https://www.4cid.org/about/) combines whole learning tasks, supportive information, procedural information, and part-task practice. This motivates returning from small capabilities to progressively more demanding versions of meaningful work. It is a framework for complex learning; tlearn uses a narrow subset rather than requiring a full program.

[Clemmons et al. (2020), BioSkills Guide](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8693931/), the competency outcome tables, grounds biological tasks in quantitative interpretation, models, evidence, and scientific communication. Its validation concerns educators' judgments of outcome relevance, not the efficacy of a particular teaching interface.

The [ISCB competency framework v3 (2024)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11646570/), competency descriptions and the data-preparation example, supports connecting biological questions, data, appropriate computation, and professional judgment. It is a community competency framework. Select relevant outcomes rather than expanding a narrow lesson into the full bioinformatics curriculum.

## Independent performance

The mastery-learning influence motivates checking readiness and repairing missing components. Our one-off tools provide local demonstrations without claiming Math Academy's mastery estimates. Manual completion, an assisted answer, an independent answer, and later retention remain distinct.

[Doignon and Falmagne (2015), Knowledge Spaces and Learning Spaces](https://arxiv.org/html/1511.06757v1), sections 1–2 and 11, define items as problem types, exercises as instances, and knowledge structures as families of feasible states. The formal theory and its assessment procedures require more than a prerequisite diagram. tlearn uses the item/instance distinction and goal-relevant dependencies to organize instruction; it does not enumerate or validate knowledge states, estimate a frontier from checkboxes, or implement adaptive diagnosis.

The [Vanderbilt knowledge-spaces project](https://github.com/vanderbilt-data-science/knowledge-spaces) provides pipeline inspiration. Its broader assessment and class-planning features are not requirements for tlearn. Preserve alternative instructional routes where justified, and label inferred prerequisites as pedagogical hypotheses.
